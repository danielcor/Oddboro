- [office] **Decision (2026-11-16): weather is off until the owner turns it on in Setup's Weather section with a country and a postal code; the office looks the code up on this computer in that country's GeoNames file, keeps only the place and its coordinates rounded to one decimal in `.env`, asks MET Norway for the forecast from its own server with one cache for every window that honours Expires and If-Modified-Since, and shows it as a pill beside the desk count.** Hard rule 4: nothing leaves until the owner says yes, and then the least that works: the country to GeoNames once (the postal code never leaves) and about 11 km of precision to MET Norway, never more often than its answer allows and never while no page is seen. Both are free for commercial use under CC BY 4.0, and both are credited in the pill and the privacy policy. One server cache means a second window or the Mac app adds no request, and a page asks only when the last answer said the next is due, on the clock it already has. The pill sits in the floor's top row rather than the office plate, so the plate gains no line, and a failed check keeps the last report with a quiet mark and its reason. The floor draws each sky from MET Norway's symbol over whatever floor is loaded, animated on the ambient clock from one fixed pool, with no flashing under reduced motion. Code in `apps/office/src/lib/server/weather.ts`, `postal-codes.ts`, `src/lib/weather.ts` and `src/lib/floor/sky.ts`; detail in `apps/office/CLAUDE.md`, under "Weather".
