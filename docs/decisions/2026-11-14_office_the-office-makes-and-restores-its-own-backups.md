- [office] **Decision (2026-11-14): the office makes and restores its own backups. Back up this office, in Setup, makes one file of the whole office except its sign-ins: a gzipped tar of a manifest, the office's own database as PGlite copies it while it runs, and the office's own folders. Restore from a backup, in Setup and on a new office's first card, checks the whole file before anything changes, saves the office as it is into `backups/before-restore-<time>`, and puts the backup in place as the office starts again, before the database opens. The file carries no password (the owner's call, 2026-11-14).** Telling owners which files to copy by hand (PR #237) asked them to get a live database and their sign-ins right themselves. A file that is not a backup, is damaged, comes from a newer Oddboro, or names a path outside the office's own folders or anything but a file or folder is refused with nothing changed. A restore grants nothing: access, levels and asks come back as the backup holds them, and every AI tool signs in again. An office on a database `.env` names backs that up with its own tools. What goes in and what stays out is in `packages/ledger/src/office-backup.ts`; the page's side and the restart are in `apps/office/src/lib/server/office-backup.ts`.
